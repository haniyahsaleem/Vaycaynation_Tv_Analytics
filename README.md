# VaycayNation: TV Campaign Performance Analytics 
### Harvard Business School Case Study | MSc Business Analytics, Qatar University

## Business Problem
German travel start-up with a 90% conversion rate among aware customers 
but only 10% brand awareness. Objective: identify what drives incremental 
website traffic lift from TV advertising to inform summer campaign strategy.

## Approach
- **Dataset:** 123 ad airings across a 5-week period (Dec 2017 – Jan 2018)
- **Variables:** 7 creative spots, 3 channels, 20 programme genres, time of day, day of week
- **Methods:** ANOVA, multiple regression, feature engineering, dummy variable encoding
- **Tools:** SPSS, Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint

## Key Findings
- Programme genre explains ~50% of lift variance — a larger effect than channel or creative spot
- Prime time (20:00–22:00) adds +50 incremental visits per airing (p = .022)
- VOX outperforms all channels; RTL consistently underperforms
- Spot 6 is the strongest creative; Spot 7 is the weakest across both measurement windows
- Projected €6,300 revenue per optimised airing based on conversion modelling

## Recommendations
1. Deploy Spot 6 as primary creative execution
2. Prioritise prime time slots on Friday–Sunday
3. Concentrate budget on VOX and Kabel1; avoid RTL
4. Target Romance and Family programme genres for highest lift
